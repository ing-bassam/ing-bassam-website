Mould survey in Berlin and Brandenburg.

Establishing the cause of mould – thermal bridge, moisture from the structure or use – by measurement and calculation. The report includes a remediation recommendation.

Who it is for

Tenants

Dispute over ventilation or building defect. The survey gives a reasoned technical answer that both sides can check, also for a lawyer or tenants' association (Mieterverein).

Landlords and property managers

Recurring mould or several affected flats. The report states the cause, the order of repairs and a cost range.

Buyers

Mould at a viewing or in the cellar. The survey clarifies before purchase whether ventilation or the building is the cause, alone or as part of a pre-purchase survey.

Scope of the survey

  • The growth – location, extent, relation to the construction; use class of the rooms according to the UBA mould guideline
  • Construction and thermal bridges – wall build-up, junctions, calculation of the temperature factor (minimum thermal protection to DIN 4108-2)
  • Indoor climate – temperature and humidity at the visit; on request logging over two to four weeks
  • Surface temperatures and dew point – where the humidity at the surface reaches the critical 80 %
  • Moisture in the structure – moisture inside walls or floors as an indication of a leak, rising damp or failed waterproofing
  • Heating and ventilation – heating pattern, window ventilation, ventilation system, furniture against external walls
  • Documents and history – year of construction, insulation, replaced windows, earlier repairs
  • Further methods – thermography, samples and microbiological analysis via specialist firms and laboratories, charged separately

What you receive

A written report with findings and photos, the cause or causes, the split between building and use, a remediation recommendation in order, a cost range and advice on heating and ventilation. It can be used as a party expert report in a dispute.

Limits of the service

The office does not carry out remediation and does not refer remediation companies. Legal questions – rent reduction (Mietminderung), cost sharing, deadlines – are for a lawyer. The result is fixed once the measurements are evaluated, not before.

How it works

  1. Enquiry. Describe the growth by email, with photos if available. You receive an assessment of which scope fits and a written quote.
  2. Site visit. 1.5 to 2.5 hours: record the growth, measure indoor climate, surface temperatures and moisture, note the construction. Data loggers are left for long-term measurement if needed.
  3. Evaluation. Calculation of the thermal bridge, comparison with the measured climate, assessment against the standards and the UBA mould guideline.
  4. Report and debrief. Within seven working days of the visit, or after the end of long-term logging. Findings, cause and remediation are then discussed.

Frequently asked questions

Building defect or poor ventilation – can a survey decide?

The survey calculates whether the wall reaches the required surface temperature and compares this with the indoor climate that actually occurred. Where both causes play a part, the report states the proportions with reasons. Where the measurements do not support a clear conclusion, the report says so.

What should I do before the visit?

Do not clean, paint over or move furniture away. Photographs with dates, notes on heating and ventilation, and documents on year of construction and earlier repairs are useful. Access to all rooms of the flat is required.

Will the report help with a rent reduction or in court?

A party expert report is the submission of the party that presents it; a court usually appoints its own expert. Rent reduction (Mietminderung), cost sharing and deadlines are matters for a lawyer. The report provides the technical basis, with findings, methods and limits.

May I remove the mould myself?

According to the mould guideline of the German Environment Agency (UBA), small, superficial growth up to about half a square metre can be removed by healthy people. Larger areas, growth inside the structure and rooms used by vulnerable people are a matter for a specialist company. The cause should be established first.

Does insurance pay for the survey?

Building insurance usually pays only if the mould results from an insured event such as a burst pipe. In that case a water damage survey applies. Mould caused by thermal bridges or by use is usually not insured.
