Condition survey · Berlin and Brandenburg

Condition survey in Berlin and Brandenburg.

Recording the existing condition (Beweissicherung) before demolition, excavation, piling or repairs: cracks with width, photos with references, a dated report.

Who it is for

Developers and contractors

Building in a perimeter block, excavating next to an old building or driving sheet piles. The record serves as the condition record under § 3(4) VOB/B and as a technical basis for claims under § 909 BGB.

Neighbours

A construction site is starting next door. The condition of the building is recorded before the first vibrations, whether or not the developer commissions a survey.

Owners, property managers, insurers

Before repairing damage, before acceptance or before building parts are closed up (§ 4(10) VOB/B), the evidence is secured.

Scope of documentation

  • Cracks – course, width measured with a crack gauge, offset of the crack edges; cracks over 0.2 mm recorded individually
  • Deformation and settlement – visible deformation of building parts, on request with levelling by a surveyor
  • Surfaces – detached plaster, loose linings and floor finishes, hollow-sounding tiles by sampling
  • Windows, doors, shutters – sample check for impaired function; broken glass
  • Damp and efflorescence – damp patches, salt efflorescence, traces of mould with location and extent
  • External areas and streets – façades, boundary walls, pavements and driveways in the construction area (§ 3(4) VOB/B)
  • Inaccessible areas – marked as such in the report
  • Additional services – crack monitors, vibration monitoring to DIN 4150-3 and level surveys via specialist firms, charged separately

What you receive

A report with instruction, date, attendees, scope, method, findings and the limits of the inspection. It contains a site plan with compass directions, room sketches with numbered walls, and every finding with the same reference in plan, text and photo. It is a record that remains readable years later without an oral explanation. Digital and on paper; the final survey compares both conditions.

Limits of the service

A condition survey records a condition, not a cause. Causes are established in a separate service. The client organises access to other people's buildings; a letter template is provided. Liability and compensation are for a lawyer.

How it works

  1. Define the scope. Construction works, affected buildings, number of visits, additional services. Gaps in the scope cannot be closed later.
  2. Organise access. A letter template for neighbours and tenants is provided; for larger projects the appointments can be coordinated on request.
  3. Initial survey before works start. All accessible surfaces inside and outside, finding by finding with reference, measurement and photo. Report within five working days.
  4. Final survey. After completion with the same grid; an interim visit if something happens during construction. The report states changes with measurements.

Frequently asked questions

Does the neighbour have to let us into their building?

No, there is no right to that. A letter template is provided. If access is refused, what can be seen from publicly accessible areas is documented and the refusal is recorded in the report.

Condition survey or independent evidence proceedings?

A condition survey is a private record without a court. Independent evidence proceedings (selbständiges Beweisverfahren) under sections 485 et seq. of the German Code of Civil Procedure (ZPO) are court proceedings with a different procedural effect. Which route suits a situation is a question for a lawyer.

When should the survey be commissioned?

Before the first intervention: before demolition, excavation, shoring, piling or the strip-out after damage. One to two weeks are needed to organise access to neighbouring buildings. Once works have started, the record is of limited use.

Does the survey replace the condition record under the VOB/B?

It puts it into practice. Section 3(4) VOB/B requires a record of the condition of roads, ground surface and structures in the construction area before work begins. The report is structured so that client and contractor can both sign it.
