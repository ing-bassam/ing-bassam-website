Construction disputes · Berlin and Brandenburg

Expert reports for construction disputes in Germany.

Technical statements on defects, damage, variations and delay, and review of existing expert reports. For law firms and parties, in English or German.

Who it is for

Law firms

Technical substance for pleadings: defect symptoms, causes, costs with a range and questions for the evidence order. The legal assessment stays with the lawyer.

International clients

Investors, developers and contractors in a German construction dispute. The technical position is delivered in English and in German, consistent in content.

Contractors and designers

Alleged defects, rejected variations, disputed extension of time. Review against the generally accepted rules of technology (anerkannte Regeln der Technik) and a calculated delay analysis.

Services

  • Expert statement – findings, cause, remedy and cost for defects and damage, with references to standards and photos
  • Review of existing expert reports – court-appointed or party reports: method, standards, gaps; objections and supplementary questions under § 411(4) ZPO
  • Questions for the evidence order – worded technically; whether the application is made is for the law firm to decide
  • Support in independent evidence proceedings – attendance at the court expert's site visit
  • Delay and disruption – extension of time, variations in principle and amount, additional costs
  • Cost estimates – remedial costs with a range to DIN 276, review of quotes and invoices
  • Condition record before repairs – see condition survey
  • Explanation at hearings – as technical adviser of the party at the hearing or in arbitration

What you receive

A statement that separates findings from assessment, cites standards and sources, contains photos and cost estimates with ranges and states the limits of the investigation. A short memo with the result precedes it. In English, German or both.

Limits of the service

The office gives no legal advice and writes no reports to order. A statement states what can be supported technically. Anyone who has acted for a party is not available as court-appointed expert in the same proceedings.

How it works

  1. Conflict check and documents. Parties, property, stage of the proceedings; file, photos, existing reports. Scope, cost cap and delivery date are stated.
  2. Site visit where needed. In pending evidence proceedings coordinated with the other side; otherwise with the client. Findings with measurements and photos.
  3. Statement. A draft goes to the law firm for technical queries; questions of understanding are answered, the result is not influenced. Then the final version.
  4. Support in the proceedings. List of questions, the court expert's site visit, statement on the court expert's report, explanation at the hearing.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly is a statement delivered?

Short statements usually follow within ten working days of receiving the documents, statements with a site visit within ten working days of the visit. For matters with a deadline, the office states at the outset whether and by when it can deliver.

Can the office also be the court-appointed expert in the same case?

No. Anyone who has acted for a party cannot be appointed by the court in the same proceedings without giving rise to concerns of bias. Prior involvement is checked before any instruction is accepted.

What is a party expert report worth in German proceedings?

It is a qualified submission of the party, which the court must take note of and consider. Whether its costs are recoverable depends on the case and is a question for the lawyer.

Is work on arbitrations in English possible?

Yes. In arbitration the parties agree the language of the proceedings, often English in international disputes. Party expert reports are prepared in English and can be explained at the hearing.

Are instructions outside Berlin accepted?

File reviews and statements on existing reports are possible anywhere in Germany. Site visits and hearings take place in Berlin and Brandenburg, elsewhere by arrangement with travel costs.
