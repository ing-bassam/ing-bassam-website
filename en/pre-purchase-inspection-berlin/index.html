Pre-purchase building survey · Berlin

Pre-purchase inspection of flats and houses in Berlin.

A civil engineer inspects the flat or house before you buy. The service covers the inspection, a document review and a written condition report with a defect list.

Scope of services

Other common terms are building survey, pre-purchase survey and home inspection. The German term is Kaufbegutachtung; in everyday usage it is also called Kaufberatung. The inspection itself and all calls can be in English.

Flat (condominium) including the owners’ association records

Included in the service

  • Review of the owners’ association (WEG) records: declaration of division, minutes, budget, accounts
  • Inspection of the flat and the accessible common property (approx. 2–3 h)
  • Verbal summary on site
  • Written condition report with defect list
  • Assessment of the maintenance reserve and the cost share
  • Duties after purchase

House detached, semi-detached or terraced

Included in the service

  • Preparation and document review
  • Inspection from roof to basement as far as accessible (approx. 4–5 h)
  • Verbal summary on site
  • Written condition report with defect list
  • Duties after purchase

We send you prices and the list of services after your enquiry by email. It shows the basic package for the property, any surcharges and all options with their price. Options include a repair cost estimate, thermal imaging, drone images or a review of the building file.

Outside Berlin the travel costs are stated in advance in the quote.

How it works

  1. Enquiry by email – Property, location and planned notary date by email; prices and the list of services follow.
  2. Appointment usually within 1–2 weeks – Access is arranged with the agent, or by the office.
  3. Inspection 2–5 h – Verbal summary on site.
  4. Report 7–10 working days – Express in 3 working days, in time for the notary.

What you receive

  • Verbal summary right after the inspection
  • Condition report as PDF with photos
  • Defect list with traffic lights: urgent · medium-term · monitor
  • Duties after purchase, with deadlines
  • Notes on subsidies and next steps
  • Optional: repair cost estimate with a range per item

What is not included

  • Non-destructive and as far as accessible: no opening-up; samples only as an option and with the owner’s consent
  • Inaccessible or concealed parts (e.g. a roof without safe access, pipes inside walls) are assessed from indications only
  • No function test of heating, electrics or pipes (done by specialist firms) and no structural assessment
  • Moisture readings are comparative; the optional cost estimate gives ranges, not quotes
  • No valuation and no legal advice – legal questions, e.g. on the purchase contract, are for a lawyer

Frequently asked questions

What is a pre-purchase inspection?

A civil engineer inspects the flat or house before the purchase contract is signed. The inspection assesses the condition of the building, lists visible defects and damage and ranks them by urgency. It serves a similar purpose to a building survey in the UK or a home inspection in the US. The German term is Kaufbegutachtung, in everyday usage also Kaufberatung; a surveyor is a Baugutachter or Bausachverständiger.

What does the inspection cover for a house or a flat?

The documents and the building are checked. For a house, the inspection runs from roof to basement as far as accessible. For a flat (apartment), it covers the flat and the accessible common property. The records of the owners’ association (WEG) are reviewed as well: declaration of division, minutes, budget and accounts. The inspection is non-destructive; concealed parts are assessed from indications.

When should the inspection take place?

Before the notary appointment, so that the findings are available before you sign. The enquiry should state the planned notary date. The verbal summary follows right after the inspection. The written report follows within 7–10 working days, or within 3 working days as an express report.

How long does the pre-purchase inspection take?

Approx. 2–3 hours for a flat and approx. 4–5 hours for a house, with a verbal summary on site. The written report follows within 7–10 working days, or within 3 working days as an express report. An appointment is usually available within 1–2 weeks.

Is attendance at the inspection required?

No. If the client is not on site, the summary is discussed afterwards by phone or video, and the condition report follows as a PDF with photos.

Does the inspection replace a valuation or legal advice?

No. The market value is not determined and no legal advice is given; legal questions, for example on the purchase contract, are for a lawyer. A valuation (in German: Wertgutachten or Verkehrswertgutachten) is not part of the service.

Does the report include an estimate of repair costs?

As an option. The repair cost estimate gives a range per item for the defects found. It does not replace quotes from contractors. It is not part of the basic package; the quote states the price for it.

Is there a shorter option without a full report?

Yes. The office also offers on-site buying advice (Kaufberatung vor Ort). It is a joint viewing of up to 2.5 hours with a verbal assessment and a photo protocol. By contrast, the pre-purchase inspection covers the document review and the written condition report with a defect list. Both are described on the page on technical due diligence.

Who commissions and pays for the inspection?

The prospective buyer. You instruct the office directly, pay the fee and receive the report. Only access to the property is arranged with the agent or the seller. The office does not broker, does not build and is not paid by the seller.

What does the pre-purchase inspection cost?

We send you prices and the list of services after your enquiry by email. The quote shows the basic package for the property, any surcharges and the options. There is no fee scale for privately instructed experts in Germany; the fee is agreed freely. Nothing is commissioned until you accept the quote. Is the report available in English?

On request. In English the condition report comes with a glossary of the German terms (Hausgeld, maintenance reserve, declaration of division).

How is the instruction given, and is withdrawal possible?

The quote comes first, with terms and information on the right of withdrawal. The instruction is then given by email; if the inspection is to take place within the 14-day withdrawal period, this must be confirmed expressly.
