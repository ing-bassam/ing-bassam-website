Duties after purchase

          

            Insulation duty after purchaseHouse · Built up to 2001
            
If the floor to the unheated loft lacks minimum insulation and the roof is uninsulated, it must be insulated (U ≤ 0.24) – within 2 years of purchase if the seller had lived there since 2002, otherwise immediately. The same applies to exposed heating pipes in unheated rooms. We check both.
Source: §§ 35, 69 GModG (vormals §§ 47, 69 GEG)

          

          

            Insulation duty of the associationFlat · Built up to 1994
            
In a block of flats, an uninsulated top floor slab must be insulated – this is the owners’ association’s job. If it is still open, you will pay a share.
Source: § 35 GModG

          

          

            Gas or oil: no forced replacementHouse and flat · Heating: Gas central heating, Gas boiler in the flat or Oil heating
            
Existing heating may keep running; the former duty to replace old boilers has gone. Anyone installing a new gas or oil heating after 29 July 2026 needs a share of biomethane, bio-oil or hydrogen from 2029: 10% (2029), 15% (2030), 30% (2035), 60% (2040). Solar thermal or a hybrid heat pump count towards it.
Source: § 43 GModG, Stand Oktober 2026

          

          

            Heating check by 30 Sept 2027Flat · Heating: Gas central heating, Oil heating, District heating or unknown · Building with 6 or more flats
            
In buildings with 6 or more flats, water-based heating installed before October 2009 must be checked and optimised by 30 Sept 2027 – the association’s duty. We look for a resolution on it.
Source: § 60b GModG

          

          

            Remote-read meters by 31 Dec 2026Flat · Heating: Gas central heating, Oil heating, District heating or unknown
            
Heat-cost allocators and water meters must be remotely readable by the end of 2026. Otherwise tenants may cut their heating costs by 3%.
Source: §§ 5, 12 HeizkostenV

          

          

            Lead pipes possibleHouse and flat · Built up to 1973
            
Lead water pipes were installed until the 1970s. The deadline for removing or disconnecting them passed on 12 Jan 2026; remaining pipes must be replaced immediately unless the health authority has extended the deadline. We inspect visible pipes; a water analysis gives certainty.
Source: § 17 TrinkwV

          

          

            Wood or tiled stoveHouse and flat · Heating: Wood, pellet or tiled stove
            
Wood or tiled stove installed before 22 March 2010: without proof of emission limits no longer permitted since 2025 – except e.g. masonry heaters, open fireplaces or a stove that is the only heating. Please ask for the chimney sweep’s certificate.
Source: § 26 1. BImSchV

          

          

            Oil tankHouse · Heating: Oil heating
            
With the purchase you become the tank operator; depending on size and location, expert inspections are mandatory. Ask for the last inspection report – decommissioning later costs money.
Source: § 46 AwSV

          

          

            Smoke alarmsHouse and flat · Location: Berlin (former West) or Berlin (former East)
            
In Berlin, smoke alarms are required in living rooms and bedrooms (not kitchens) and hallways, including existing homes.
Source: § 48 Abs. 4 BauO Bln

          

        

        

          
Letting

          

            What fittings add to the rentFlat · Built up to 2024 · Use: Let it out
            
According to the guidance for Berlin’s 2026 rent index, each feature group shifts the rent by 20% of the gap between the mean and the upper (or lower) value. Example 57 m² period flat in Neukölln: if the “building” group turns positive, e.g. via a video intercom, and the other groups balance out, about €40–47 more per month is possible.
Matching option: Rental check
Source: Berliner Mietspiegel 2026, Orientierungshilfe

          

          

            New build from 2025Flat · Built from 2025 · Use: Let it out
            
The 2026 rent index does not apply to flats completed from 2025. The comparative rent can only be determined via comparable flats or an expert opinion.
Source: Berliner Mietspiegel 2026, Nr. 3

          

          

            Rent brake until 2029House and flat · Use: Let it out
            
On re-letting, at most 10% above the comparative rent (Berlin, until 31 Dec 2029); rent increases at most 15% in 3 years. Exception: first letting after 1 Oct 2014. Legal details: ask a landlord association or lawyer.
Source: §§ 556d ff., 558 BGB; Berliner Verordnungen

          

          

            Check the living areaHouse and flat · Use: Let it out or It is let – I want to move in later
            
If the area is more than 10% smaller than in the tenancy agreement, the rent is reduced. We check the stated area for plausibility.
Source: BGH, VIII ZR 295/03

          

          

            Social preservation areaFlat · In a social preservation area (Milieuschutz)
            
Upgrades need a permit here. Video intercom, underfloor heating or a second bathroom are usually not permitted – this limits upgrades and rental potential.
Source: Berliner Genehmigungskriterien soziale Erhaltungsgebiete (2026)

          

          

            Own-use notice may be barredFlat · Use: It is let – I want to move in later
            
If the flat was converted into a condominium after the tenancy began, an own-use notice is barred for 10 years in Berlin. A sale does not end a tenancy. Have this checked legally before the notary appointment.
Source: § 577a BGB; Berliner Kündigungsschutzklausel-Verordnung

          

          

            Duties as a landlordHouse and flat · Use: Let it out
            
Show the energy certificate at viewings, split CO2 costs by the tiered model, smoke alarms in living rooms and hallways. With a new gas or oil heating you will also share costs from 2028.
Source: § 80 GModG; CO2KostAufG; § 48 BauO Bln

          

        

        

          
What we look at closely

          

            Gas boiler in the flatFlat · Heating: Gas boiler in the flat
            
The former special deadlines for owners’ associations with per-flat gas boilers no longer apply. We check age, flue and whether the boiler is part of your unit.
Source: GModG, §§ 71l, 71n aufgehoben

          

          

            Night-storage heatersHouse and flat · Built up to 1984 · Heating: Night-storage / electric
            
Units from before about 1984 may contain asbestos. Do not open them; removal is a job for a specialist firm. Replacing them with a heat pump is subsidised.
Source: Berliner Mieterverein, MieterMagazin 06/2011

          

          

            District heatingHouse and flat · Heating: District heating
            
We look at the substation and its age; please send the supply contract and price clause. Berlin’s heat plan shows network areas but imposes no obligations.
Source: Berliner Wärmeplan (Senat, 16.06.2026)

          

          

            Heat pumpHouse and flat · Heating: Heat pump
            
We check type, age, flow temperature and maintenance. What matters for running costs is the seasonal performance factor – please send the electricity bills.
Source: GModG § 60a

          

          

            Period building up to 1918House and flat · Built up to 1918
            
Timber-beam floors with hidden beam ends in external walls and under bathrooms can rot; dry rot is possible too. Basements often lack a damp-proof course. We look for this specifically.
Source: Fachliteratur: Stahr, Bausanierung (2022); DIN 68800-4

          

          

            Built 1919–1948House and flat · Built 1919–1948
            
Hardly any insulation, often timber-beam floors. Expect higher heating costs and a mould risk at cold corners.
Source: Fachliteratur: Stahr, Bausanierung (2022)

          

          

            Post-war buildingHouse and flat · Built 1945–1965
            
Partly rubble bricks or brick-aggregate concrete with variable strength. Have the load capacity checked before opening walls or extending.
Source: Deutscher Bundestag, Drucksache 11/179

          

          

            Asbestos possibleHouse and flat · Built up to 1993
            
Asbestos was used until 1993 (occasionally slightly later), e.g. in tile adhesive, filler, vinyl floor tiles, roof and façade panels. Usually harmless when intact – it matters when you renovate. You must then give the contractor the year of construction and any known indications of pollutants (legal duty).
Matching option: Pollutant samples with lab analysis
Source: Verbraucherzentrale; § 5a GefStoffV

          

          

            PCBs and PAHs possibleHouse and flat · Built 1950–1978
            
Flexible joint sealants may contain PCBs; black parquet adhesive and tar felt may contain PAHs. Do not sand old parquet before the adhesive has been tested.
Matching option: Pollutant samples with lab analysis
Source: Fachliteratur: Kaub, Schadstoffe im Bauwesen (2024)

          

          

            Old mineral woolHouse and flat · Built 1930–2000
            
Insulation wool installed before 1996 is classed as carcinogenic; old material may still have been used until 2000. Do not remove it yourself – only via a specialist firm with protective measures.
Source: TRGS 521 (Ausgabe Mai 2026)

          

          

            Old electrical installationHouse and flat · Built up to 1972
            
Installations from before 1973 often lack an earth conductor. Grandfathered does not mean safe – a check by an electrician is advisable.
Source: ZVEI, Elektro-Altanlagen (2020)

          

          

            Wiring over 35 years oldHouse and flat · Built 1973–1991
            
Wiring and terminals are regarded as worn after about 35 years; a residual-current device is often missing. A check by an electrician is recommended.
Source: ZVEI, Elektro-Altanlagen (2020)

          

          

            GDR-era buildingHouse and flat · Built 1949–1990 · Location: Berlin (former East) or Brandenburg
            
Possible are aluminium wiring (fire risk at terminals), asbestos cement and Sokalit boards, and roof timbers treated with Hylotox. We look out for these; only an analysis gives certainty.
Source: Umweltbundesamt, Asbest in der DDR; WD Bundestag 2002

          

          

            Prefab house of this eraHouse · Built 1956–1991 · Prefabricated / timber-frame house
            
Wood preservatives (PCP, lindane), formaldehyde and a musty smell from chloroanisoles are possible. Consider an indoor-air analysis before buying – we can arrange it via a partner lab.
Source: Verbraucherzentrale NRW, Fertighaus aus den 70er-Jahren

          

          

            Flat roofs of this eraHouse · Built 1960–1990
            
Membranes last about 30–35 years and have usually been renewed or are due. Please send records of the last renewal.
Source: BBSR, Nutzungsdauern von Bauteilen (Stand 2025)

          

          

            Riser pipesFlat · Built 1950–1980
            
Water and waste risers are often original. Riser replacement is frequently the association’s largest single cost – we look for it in the minutes.
Source: Fachliteratur; § 5 Abs. 2 WEG

          

          

            Concrete balconiesFlat · Built 1950–1995
            
Cantilevered balconies without thermal breaks often show rust and spalling. Repair can easily reach five figures per balcony; the declaration of division says who pays.
Source: Fachliteratur: Stahr, Bausanierung (2022)

          

          

            Prefabricated concrete blockFlat · Built 1965–1990 · Location: Berlin (former East)
            
We check joints, balcony slabs and external walls specifically. Many 1990s renovations (insulation, windows, risers) are approaching their second cycle – this can mean special levies.
Source: BBSR, Nutzungsdauern von Bauteilen (Stand 2025)

          

          

            First renewals dueHouse and flat · Built 1995–2009
            
Boilers (approx. 18–20 years), window seals and joints are often due at this age. Please send maintenance and replacement records.
Source: BBSR, Nutzungsdauern von Bauteilen (Stand 2025)

          

          

            Recent buildingHouse and flat · Built from 2010
            
Waterproofing defects in showers, balconies and flat roofs are common. Defect claims usually expire 5 years after acceptance – please send acceptance records and defect lists.
Source: § 634a BGB

          

          

            Thermal bridges and mouldHouse and flat · Built up to 2000
            
Before 2001 there were no uniform requirements for thermal bridges. Mould can form at corners, reveals and behind furniture, especially after new windows. We take readings at critical points.
Source: UBA-Schimmelleitfaden (2017, präzisiert 2024)

          

        

        

          
Good to know

          

            Energy-certificate consultationHouse
            
When buying a house with at most 2 dwellings, until the end of 2026 you must hold a free consultation on the energy certificate if one is offered.
Source: § 80 Abs. 4 GModG

          

          

            “Jung kauft Alt” loanHouse and flat · Built up to 1994
            
Families with a child under 18 and income up to €90,000 (plus €10,000 per further child) can get a reduced-interest KfW loan for houses and flats in energy classes F, G or H if they renovate within 4.5 years. Important: apply before the purchase contract.
Source: KfW-Programm 308 (Wohneigentum für Familien – Bestandserwerb)

          

          

            Heating replacement: bonus limitedHouse and flat · Heating: Oil heating, Gas boiler in the flat, Night-storage / electric or Gas central heating
            
Owner-occupiers replacing their heating receive grants. The 16% bonus applies only to applications by 31 Jan 2027 and then decreases step by step; an income-related bonus may be added.
Source: BEG Einzelmaßnahmen (Richtlinie vom 17.08.2026, gültig seit 21.07.2026)

          

          

            Roof renovation in BerlinHouse · Built up to 1995 · Location: Berlin (former West) or Berlin (former East)
            
Anyone in Berlin who substantially renovates the roof must install a PV system (Berlin Solar Act). Include this in your budget.
Source: Solargesetz Berlin

          

          

            Thermal imaging in winter onlyHouse and flat
            
Thermal images are only meaningful with a sufficient difference between inside and outside (rule of thumb at least 10 K, no sun) – usually November to March, early morning.
Source: DIN EN ISO 6781-1 (2023)

Frequently asked questions

      

        
        
How long does the pre-purchase inspection take?
The inspection takes approx. 2–3 hours for a flat and approx. 4–5 hours for a house; you get the verbal summary right on site. The written report follows within 7–10 working days, as an express report within 3 working days. An appointment is usually available within 1–2 weeks.

        
Do I have to be there for the inspection?
No. You arrange access with the agent – or we do. If you are not on site, we go through the summary with you afterwards by phone or video; you receive the condition report as a PDF with photos.

        
Is the report in English?
Not as standard – our reports are written in German. With the option “Report in English” for €142.80 incl. VAT (€120.00 net) you receive the condition report in English, with a glossary of the German terms (Hausgeld, maintenance reserve, declaration of division). The inspection itself and all calls can be in English.

        
Do you work outside Berlin?
Yes. Within Berlin, travel is included in the fixed price. Outside Berlin we state the travel fee in advance in our quote.

        
How do I instruct you – and can I withdraw?
You first receive our quote with our terms and information on your right of withdrawal. You then instruct us by email; if the inspection is to take place within the 14-day withdrawal period, please confirm this expressly.

        
Does the inspection replace a valuation or legal advice?
No. We do not determine the market value and do not give legal advice – legal questions, e.g. on the purchase contract, are for your lawyer. The inspection is non-destructive and covers what is accessible; costs are estimate ranges, not quotes.
