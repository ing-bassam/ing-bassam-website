Flat (condominium) including the owners’ association records

Included in the service

  • Review of the owners’ association (WEG) records: declaration of division, minutes, budget, accounts

Included in the service

  • Written condition report with defect list
  • 10–15-year cost outlook
  • Location check from official maps
  • Duties after purchase

How it works

  1. Enquiry today – Property, location and planned notary date by email – you receive prices and the list of services.
  2. Appointment usually within 1–2 weeks – You arrange access with the agent – or we do.
  3. Inspection 2–5 h – Verbal summary right on site.
  4. Report 7–10 working days – Express in 3 working days – in time for the notary.

What you receive

  • Verbal summary right after the inspection
  • Condition report as PDF with photos
  • Defect list with traffic lights: urgent · medium-term · monitor
  • Cost ranges per item and for 10–15 years
  • Duties after purchase, with deadlines
  • Notes on subsidies and next steps

What is not included

  • Non-destructive and as far as accessible: no opening-up; samples only as an option and with the owner’s consent
  • Inaccessible or concealed parts (e.g. a roof without safe access, pipes inside walls) are assessed from indications only
  • No function test of heating, electrics or pipes (done by specialist firms) and no structural assessment
  • Moisture readings are comparative; costs are estimate ranges, not quotes
  • No valuation and no legal advice – legal questions, e.g. on the purchase contract, are for your lawyer

Frequently asked questions

How long does the pre-purchase inspection take?

The inspection takes approx. 2–3 hours for a flat and approx. 4–5 hours for a house; you get the verbal summary right on site. The written report follows within 7–10 working days, as an express report within 3 working days. An appointment is usually available within 1–2 weeks.

Do I have to be there for the inspection?

No. You arrange access with the agent – or we do. If you are not on site, we go through the summary with you afterwards by phone or video; you receive the condition report as a PDF with photos.

What does the pre-purchase inspection cost?

We send you prices and the list of services after your enquiry by email – we reply within one working day. Write to info@ing-bassam.de or call +49 176 23581339. Nothing is commissioned until you accept our quote.

Is the report in English?

On request. Our reports are written in German as standard; in English you receive the condition report with a glossary of the German terms (Hausgeld, maintenance reserve, declaration of division). We state the price for this in our quote. The inspection itself and all calls can be in English.

Do you work outside Berlin?

Yes, also in the area around Berlin. Outside Berlin we state the travel costs in advance in our quote.

How do I instruct you – and can I withdraw?

You first receive our quote with our terms and information on your right of withdrawal. You then instruct us by email; if the inspection is to take place within the 14-day withdrawal period, please confirm this expressly.

Does the inspection replace a valuation or legal advice?

No. We do not determine the market value and do not give legal advice – legal questions, e.g. on the purchase contract, are for your lawyer. The inspection is non-destructive and covers what is accessible; costs are estimate ranges, not quotes.
