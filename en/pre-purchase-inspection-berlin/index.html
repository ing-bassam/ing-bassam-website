Pre-purchase inspections · Berlin

Pre-purchase inspection in Berlin.

Inspection, document review and a written condition report with a defect list and a cost outlook for the next 10 to 15 years – for flats and houses.

Enquiry by email Scope of services
  • Service area: Berlin and surroundings
  • Written condition report with photos, defect list and cost ranges
  • Report in German as standard, in English on request

Scope of services

Reports are written in German as standard; a report in English is available on request. The inspection itself and all calls can be in English.

Flat (condominium) including the owners’ association records

Included in the service

  • Review of the owners’ association (WEG) records: declaration of division, minutes, budget, accounts
  • Inspection of the flat and the accessible common property (approx. 2–3 h)
  • Verbal summary on site
  • Written condition report with defect list
  • 10–15-year cost outlook
  • Assessment of the maintenance reserve and the cost share
  • Duties after purchase

House detached, semi-detached or terraced

Included in the service

  • Preparation and document review
  • Inspection from roof to basement as far as accessible (approx. 4–5 h)
  • Verbal summary on site
  • Written condition report with defect list
  • 10–15-year cost outlook
  • Duties after purchase

We send you prices and the list of services after your enquiry by email. It shows the basic package for the property, any surcharges and all options with their price, for example thermal imaging, drone images or a review of the building file.

Outside Berlin the travel costs are stated in advance in the quote.

How it works

  1. Enquiry by email – Property, location and planned notary date by email; prices and the list of services follow.
  2. Appointment usually within 1–2 weeks – Access is arranged with the agent, or by the office.
  3. Inspection 2–5 h – Verbal summary on site.
  4. Report 7–10 working days – Express in 3 working days, in time for the notary.

What you receive

  • Verbal summary right after the inspection
  • Condition report as PDF with photos
  • Defect list with traffic lights: urgent · medium-term · monitor
  • Cost ranges per item and for 10–15 years
  • Duties after purchase, with deadlines
  • Notes on subsidies and next steps

What is not included

  • Non-destructive and as far as accessible: no opening-up; samples only as an option and with the owner’s consent
  • Inaccessible or concealed parts (e.g. a roof without safe access, pipes inside walls) are assessed from indications only
  • No function test of heating, electrics or pipes (done by specialist firms) and no structural assessment
  • Moisture readings are comparative; costs are estimate ranges, not quotes
  • No valuation and no legal advice – legal questions, e.g. on the purchase contract, are for a lawyer

Frequently asked questions

How long does the pre-purchase inspection take?

The inspection takes approx. 2–3 hours for a flat and approx. 4–5 hours for a house, with a verbal summary on site. The written report follows within 7–10 working days, as an express report within 3 working days. An appointment is usually available within 1–2 weeks.

Is attendance at the inspection required?

No. Access is arranged with the agent, or by the office. If the client is not on site, the summary is discussed afterwards by phone or video, and the condition report follows as a PDF with photos.

Is the report available in English?

On request. Reports are written in German as standard; in English the condition report comes with a glossary of the German terms (Hausgeld, maintenance reserve, declaration of division). The inspection itself and all calls can be in English.

How is the instruction given, and is withdrawal possible?

The quote comes first, with terms and information on the right of withdrawal. The instruction is then given by email; if the inspection is to take place within the 14-day withdrawal period, this must be confirmed expressly.

Does the inspection replace a valuation or legal advice?

No. The market value is not determined and no legal advice is given; legal questions, for example on the purchase contract, are for a lawyer. The inspection is non-destructive and covers what is accessible; costs are estimate ranges, not quotes.
