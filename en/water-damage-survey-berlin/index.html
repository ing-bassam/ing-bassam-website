Water damage survey in Berlin and Brandenburg.

Establishing the source of the water, the affected components and layers, and the scope of drying and reinstatement. For owners' associations, tenants, landlords and insurers.

Who it is for

Owners' associations and property managers

Several flats affected, common and private property (Gemeinschafts- und Sondereigentum) unclear, drying company and insurer disagree. The report provides findings towards owners, tenants and the insurer.

Owners and tenants

Dispute about the cause, the scope of the repair or a rent reduction. The office carries out no drying or repair works itself.

Insurers and loss adjusters

Cause, extent, necessity and reasonableness of measures and costs, with photo documentation and separation of findings and assessment. See courts and insurers (German).

Scope of the survey

  • Damage pattern and moisture distribution – affected components and layers, spread in walls, ceilings and screed, insulation layers
  • Source of the water – supply pipe, waste water, rainwater, condensation or moisture in the structure; leak detection via specialist firms if required
  • Documentation before the strip-out – the condition before floor finishes are removed and drying equipment is set up
  • Drying concept – method, duration, measurement records, success check; whether insulation-layer drying is possible
  • Scope of repair and costs – what is necessary, what goes beyond it, cost range for reinstatement
  • Consequential damage – mould, odour, load-bearing capacity of wet timber joist floors, electrical installation
  • Hazardous materials in older buildings – pointers to asbestos or man-made mineral fibres to be checked before the strip-out
  • Documents – riser plans, maintenance records, damage reports, photos from the first hours, correspondence with insurer and contractors

What you receive

A written report with findings and photos, the cause, the technical split between common and private property, an assessment of the drying and repair concept, a cost range and a check of quotes or invoices. Findings and assessment are kept separate.

Limits of the service

The office does not dry or repair and has no stake in any remediation company. Leak detection and laboratory analysis are carried out by specialist firms. Cover and settlement are decided by the insurer; claims are a matter for a lawyer.

How it works

  1. Report and documents. Describe the damage by email and send photos, damage reports and correspondence with the insurer. You are told what should stay untouched until the visit.
  2. Site visit before the strip-out. Two to three hours: record the damage pattern, measure the moisture distribution, identify components and layers, narrow down the source. In rented flats access is coordinated with the occupants.
  3. Assessment. Cause, extent, necessity; check of the drying concept, quotes and measurement records.
  4. Report and support. Report within seven working days. On request: attendance at control measurements and the acceptance of the repair, and a check of the final invoice.

Frequently asked questions

May drying start before the surveyor has been?

Limiting the damage is possible; removing the evidence is not. Turn off the water, remove standing water, photograph everything and report the damage to the insurer. Removing floor finishes, opening components and setting up drying equipment should wait until the condition is documented.

Who instructs the survey in an owners' association?

Usually the property manager on behalf of the association, because damage to common property affects several owners. Whether a resolution is needed depends on the community rules and German condominium law, which a lawyer clarifies. The report separates common and private property technically so that costs can be allocated.

The insurer is sending its own expert – is a separate report useful?

The insurer's expert works for the insurer. If the findings differ on cause, extent or necessity of measures, a separate report provides the technical counterpoint. Many policies provide for an expert procedure (Sachverständigenverfahren); a lawyer checks whether it applies.

How quickly can a visit take place?

For fresh damage usually within three working days, because the condition has to be documented before the strip-out. The enquiry should state whether drying or removal work has already been announced.

Is leak detection part of the service?

Locating hidden leaks is done by specialist firms. They are coordinated if needed, and their results are assessed in the report and related to the building components. Leak detection is charged separately.
