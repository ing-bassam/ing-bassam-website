New-build inspection and snagging in Berlin and Brandenburg.

Inspection of construction work at defined stages and a snagging list for the acceptance (Abnahme). For private clients building a house or buying from a developer (Bauträger).

Who it is for

Buyers from a developer

At acceptance, the burden of proof and the deadlines shift. The inspection takes place beforehand, defects are recorded with reservation and the appointment is attended.

Self-builders with a general contractor

A contract with specification and payment plan exists, but no check in the client's interest. The documents are read and the decisive stages are inspected.

Owners renovating

Roof, façade, bathroom, basement: the junctions with existing parts are checked before they are covered up.

Scope and timing of inspections

  • Before construction – review of contract and specification (Baubeschreibung), working drawings, soil report, thermal insulation certificate; inspection plan
  • Foundations – soil as described in the soil report, blinding layer, position of reinforcement, waterproofing, pipe penetrations
  • Shell – dimensional accuracy and flatness to DIN 18202, masonry and concrete, slabs, junctions, concrete cover
  • Building envelope – insulation, vapour control layer, window junctions, roof; airtightness to DIN 4108-7, blower-door test via a specialist firm
  • Before the screed – pipe routing on the slab, insulation layer, edge strips, waterproofing in bathrooms
  • Before acceptance – surfaces, function of building services, completeness of documents (certificates, test records, energy performance certificate)
  • Acceptance (Abnahme) – attendance, snagging list with photos and reservations, pointers to deadlines
  • Before the warranty expires – inspection some months before the end of the limitation period

What you receive

After every visit a report with construction stage, findings, assessment and recommendation, including photos of parts that will soon be covered. A defect list with urgency, an acceptance protocol and a template for the notice of defects. Questions by phone between visits are included in the new-build package.

Limits of the service

The office does not run the site and does not instruct the contractors. Legal consequences, withholding payments and deadlines are for a lawyer. Comprehensive site supervision as client's representative (HOAI work stage 8) is offered separately.

How it works

  1. Documents. Contract, specification, drawings and schedule are sent by email and reviewed for what is owed.
  2. Inspection plan. Inspection stages are fixed and the contractor is informed together with the client. Visits are called off by construction stage.
  3. Visits. Check of dimensions, junctions and details, photo documentation, measurements via specialist firms where needed. Report within three working days.
  4. Acceptance and follow-up. Pre-inspection, snagging list, attendance at the acceptance appointment; later a check of the remedial work and an inspection before the warranty expires.

Frequently asked questions

Is my own surveyor allowed on site?

Accompanied by the client, as a rule yes; many construction contracts expressly provide for access. It is advisable to inform the contractor of the planned inspection dates before construction starts.

How quickly can a visit take place?

Building parts are often finished and still open for only one or two days. In the new-build package visits are available within 48 hours of the call; single visits are booked by availability, usually within five working days. The expected construction schedule allows the windows to be planned in advance.

How does this differ from site supervision under the HOAI?

Site supervision (HOAI work stage 8) is the designer's ongoing construction management with site diary, scheduling and invoice checks. Construction quality inspection is an additional, sample-based check on behalf of the client. Comprehensive site supervision as the client's representative is offered separately by quote.

What happens if defects are found?

The report states the finding with photo, assessment and recommendation. The defect has to be notified in writing to the contractual partner; the notice-of-defects generator produces the German letter. Whether payments may be withheld is a question for a lawyer.

Is an acceptance inspection useful when buying from a developer?

With acceptance the burden of proof shifts, and known defects have to be reserved in the protocol. With a developer, the unit and the common property are accepted separately. Both appointments can be attended.
